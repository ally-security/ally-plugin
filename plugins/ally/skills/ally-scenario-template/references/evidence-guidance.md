# Credible Scenario Template Evidence

## Evidence levels

Prefer this source order:

1. Victim disclosure, regulator/government finding, court record, official
   advisory, or vendor notice.
2. Direct forensic, incident-response, or reproducible technical analysis.
3. Rigorous independent reporting with documents or named sources.
4. Aggregators, social posts, forums, attacker claims, and unsourced summaries
   as discovery leads only.

For a known-breach template, use at least three retrieved Tier 1-3 sources,
including one Tier 1 source. Central claims should have two independent
provenance chains where the public record permits it. Articles repeating the
same victim notice are one chain, not independent corroboration.

## Claim discipline

Track each central claim as one atomic statement with its sources and status:
verified, strongly supported, disputed, or unknown. Participant-facing facts
must be verified or strongly supported. Exclude disputed and unknown claims
rather than smoothing over contradictions.

Distinguish four clocks when relevant: event, detection, disclosure, and source
publication. Use ranges when evidence is imprecise. Never turn a month-only
source into an exact date.

## Transforming evidence into an exercise

Classify each scenario element as preserved, generalized, compressed,
composite, or invented. Invented pressure can create a decision deadline or
synthetic message, but must not add a new attack mechanism, affected data class,
legal duty, actor attribution, victim action, or material consequence.

Keep historical names only in real-breach metadata and evidence notes. Make
playable scenarios organization-neutral while preserving the verified causal
mechanism. Use synthetic media clearly as exercise fiction.

## ATT&CK

Map behavior, not tools, outcomes, or actor reputation. Check the current
official Enterprise technique page, prefer a sub-technique only when the
evidence supports the added specificity, and use an empty list for pure
business, legal, recovery, or communications injects.
